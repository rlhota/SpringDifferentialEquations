Mass-Spring Simulation (Ideal System)

This activity demonstrates how the second-order differential equation
m d²y/dt² + k y = 0 models the motion of a mass on a spring.

Guiding Questions:

  1. What happens to the oscillation frequency when you increase the mass?
  2. What happens when you increase the spring constant?
  3. Compare the simulation to the analytical solution: y(t) = A cos(ωt) + B sin(ωt). How does ω = √(k/m) affect the motion?
  4. Can you predict the period of oscillation from ω?
